Notchy — a living notch for Windows
===================================

Getting started
---------------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you keep apps, for example
   C:\Users\<you>\Apps\Notchy  (not inside Downloads, not inside the ZIP).
2. Double-click Notchy.exe. A small notch appears at the top centre of your screen.
3. Hover or click the notch to open the panel. Right-click it for Settings.

"Windows protected your PC"
---------------------------
This build is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the
first time. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It only asks once.

Start with Windows
------------------
Settings > Behaviour > "Launch at startup".

The taskbar
-----------
Notchy can replace the Windows taskbar with its own bar. It is OFF at first.
Turn it on in Settings > Taskbar if you want it.

If anything goes wrong, start Notchy with the --safe-mode option (for example from a
shortcut: Notchy.exe --safe-mode). It keeps the normal taskbar and turns optional
features off, without changing your settings.

Your data
---------
Settings and data live in  %USERPROFILE%\.winisland  on your PC only. Screen time,
clipboard history, weather and AI usage are off until you turn them on.

Removing it
-----------
Installed with Notchy-Setup: Settings > Apps > Notchy > Uninstall.
Portable ZIP: quit Notchy (right-click the notch > Exit), turn off "Launch at startup" first if you
turned it on, then delete this folder. To also remove your settings, delete
%USERPROFILE%\.winisland.
